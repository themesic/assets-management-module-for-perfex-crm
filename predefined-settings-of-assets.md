# Predefined settings of Assets

This includes managing assets subcategories, units of measurement, and locations to ensure proper organization and tracking of all your business assets.

### 1. Accessing Assets Predefined Settings

<figure><img src=".gitbook/assets/Screenshot from 2025-06-03 16-55-21.png" alt=""><figcaption><p>Assets Setting</p></figcaption></figure>

#### Navigation

**From Main Menu**: Go to Settings → Assets Settings to access all asset configuration options

**From Dashboard**: Click on the Assets Settings tile or widget if available on your dashboard

**Quick Access**: Some asset settings may be accessible directly from asset creation forms

#### Settings Overview

The Assets Settings section provides centralized management for:

* **Subcategories**: Detailed assets subdivisions
* **Units**: Measurement units for assets quantities
* **Locations**: Physical locations where assets are stored or used
